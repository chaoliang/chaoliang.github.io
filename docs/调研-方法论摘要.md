## methodology
Programmatic SEO 2024-2026: Google's March 2024 spam policy ("scaled content abuse"), the Aug 2025 spam update, and the March 2026 core update algorithmically filter variable-injection templates (60-90% traffic loss, no manual action, no reconsideration), while dataset-driven pages survive. The line: every page must answer a genuinely distinct query with data not obtainable by swapping one variable. Practitioner heuristics: 30-40%+ per-page uniqueness ratio, conditional template sections, 100-150 words of per-page editorial synthesis, data tables/charts as the core block. Ian Nuttall ($26k/mo pSEO portfolio) now builds clusters of ~2,000 pages max with dense internal linking, sitemaps capped at 10k URLs, aged domains when possible. Indexing at 1000+ pages: batch publishing (first batch ≤50, validate 3-4 weeks, then 10x phases), internal links beat sitemap entries; Google Indexing API is restricted (JobPosting only); IndexNow covers Bing. Internal linking: hub-spoke with bidirectional links plus 1-2 sibling spokes, ≤3 clicks depth. New domains: only 1.74% of new pages hit top-10 within a year, but near-zero-competition long-tails rank in days (matches datesum's day-8 click). KGR (allintitle/volume<0.25, volume<250) still works at volume. AI Overviews cut informational CTR ~61%; interactive calculators/tools are the most resistant page class — datesum's model remains the right one.
- Punished (Mar 2024/Aug 2025/Mar 2026 updates): pure template+variable substitution ('best X in [city]'), mass AI pages without editorial review, and aggregation without synthesis (even LLM-rewritten). Penalty is algorithmic, sitewide, 60-90% loss within ~14 days, no reconsideration path — recovery takes 3-6 months across core-update cycles.
- Survivor pattern: each page answers a distinct query from a dataset with a mandatory 'unique data' column. Heuristic: uniqueness ratio ≥30-40% (an 800-word page with 750 boilerplate words = 6% = high risk). Add 100-150 words of page-specific synthesis and conditional sections that render only when data exists.
- Template structure that works: dynamic title/H1/meta from variables, varied intro framing, data tables/charts/comparisons as core content block, per-page FAQs, page-type schema. Seomatic's 4-gate test before building: (1) demand across 50+ variations, (2) unique data per variation, (3) weak SERP (directories/aggregators, not editorial), (4) capacity to maintain/refresh the dataset.
- Ian Nuttall playbook ($26k/mo solo pSEO portfolio, x.com/iannuttall/status/1748286385797153041): topical clusters of ≤2,000 pages at a time (his billions-of-URLs visualfractions approach is 'now considered excessive'), XML sitemaps capped at 10k URLs, aged/abandoned domains (~$2k) to skip trust window, combine multiple data sources, AI writes around the dataset (not freeform), traffic→email→products beyond ads, code > no-code.
- Indexing 1000+ pages: never publish more than ~50 pages in the first batch; monitor GSC 3-4 weeks; target >70% indexation at 4 weeks before next wave; scale in ~10x phases (1,000 → validate 6-8 weeks → more). Publishing fewer pages at a time indexes the full set faster. Fix 'Discovered - currently not indexed' by unpublishing, improving, republishing in 50-page batches at 2-3 week intervals.
- Indexing tools reality: Google Indexing API is limited to JobPosting/BroadcastEvent pages (200 URLs/day; abuse risks revocation after Google's May 2025 clarification). IndexNow is push-indexing for Bing/Yandex/Naver only — Google never adopted it. Practitioners use urlmonitor.com (Nuttall's own tool) plus GSC URL Inspection (~10-12 manual requests/day). An internal link from an indexed page is a stronger crawl signal than any sitemap entry.
- Internal linking architecture: hub-and-spoke with bidirectional links (spoke→hub reinforces topical authority) plus spoke-to-spoke 'related pages' modules of 3-5 contextually-similar pages (relevance scored by shared attributes); everything ≤3 clicks from homepage; tiered link budgets ~50-100 links on hubs, 20-50 on subcategory pages, 5-15 on leaf pages. Post-2026, internal links FROM thin pages are discounted — link density can't fake authority anymore.
- New-domain ranking speed 2025-2026: Ahrefs data — only 1.74% of new pages reach top-10 within a year (down from 5.7% in 2017), average #1 result is ~5 years old; BUT near-zero-competition long-tails rank in days-to-weeks even on fresh domains (consistent with datesum: indexed in 10 days, first click day 8). Expect a 2-6 month trust-evaluation window with volatile rankings for anything contested.
- KGR still works: allintitle-results ÷ monthly-volume < 0.25 with volume <250 = rankable in days for new sites (Doug Cunnington's original case: 200 KGR posts in 5 months, $100→$14k/mo). Requires volume of pages to matter. Modern complement: mine zero-volume keywords via GSC impression data, People Also Ask, and autocomplete — tools under-report hyper-specific queries; long-tail = ~70% of all search traffic.
- AI Overviews are the new tax on informational pSEO: on ~48% of queries by 2026, 36% trigger rate on informational queries, organic CTR -61% where present (1.76%→0.61%); comparison-format queries hit hardest (95% AIO coverage). Interactive calculators/tools/utility pages are the most AIO-resistant class because the answer requires interaction — validates the datesum model over prose-answer pages. Adsterra case study confirms long-tail informational queries 'dropping out of SERPs' since 2025.
- Stop-scaling red flags (SEOTakeoff): sustained indexation <20%, >60% of pages getting <1 session/month, >20% of pages flagged 'Duplicate, not selected as canonical', <20% of pages driving >80% of traffic → prune (301 to nearest relevant page or 410; better than leaving thin pages live).
- Actionable synthesis for the next datesum-style site: pick a deterministic-computation query pattern (dates/units/numbers) where each page's answer is mathematically unique; build the interlinked cluster (hub + related-pages module) BEFORE launch; ship ≤50 pages first, validate indexation, then scale in waves; keep sitemaps ≤10k URLs with honest lastmod; target KGR/zero-volume long-tails; prefer tool/calculator output over prose that AI Overviews can absorb.

## ctr
Rich-result landscape has narrowed hard: FAQ (2023) and HowTo (2023) rich results are gone, Sitelinks search box died Nov 2024, seven more types retired June 2025, Practice Problems ends Jan 2026, Dataset now only feeds Dataset Search, Speakable remains news-only beta. What still renders: Breadcrumb, Article, Organization, Product/Review stars, Video, Software App (SoftwareApplication/WebApplication — the best fit for calculator pages), and Math Solver. Measured CTR levers: question-word titles +14.1%, 40-60-char titles +8.9%, keyword-in-URL +45%, meta description present +5.8%, power words -13.9% (Backlinko); current-year-in-title refreshes lifted CTR 37-640% in a 10-article case study, but Google's Dec 2025 core update discounts date-only fake freshness. Featured snippets earn ~42.9% CTR but SERP visibility fell 64% in H1 2025; they remain the top path into AI Overview citations. PAA wants exact-question H2s with 40-60-word answers. Discover is auto-eligible (needs 1200px images, max-image-preview:large; ~8% CTR) but favors editorial content, not tools. GEO is now material: AI referrals hit 1.13B visits/June 2025 (+357% YoY), ChatGPT drives ~87% of them and converts at 15.9% vs ~1.8% organic; Bing indexing feeds ChatGPT; stats/citations/Q&A formatting lift AI citation odds 25-40%.
- Schema that still pays for a calculator site: SoftwareApplication/WebApplication (Software App rich result with ratings), BreadcrumbList, Article (for guides), Organization, and Math Solver (still active per Google's gallery). Dead: FAQ + HowTo rich results (2023), Sitelinks search box (Nov 21, 2024), ClaimReview/Course Info/Estimated Salary/Learning Video/Special Announcement/Vehicle Listing/Book Actions (June 2025), Practice Problems (Jan 2026); Dataset markup now surfaces only in Dataset Search; Speakable is still a news-only beta. Keep schema anyway - it aids AI/entity comprehension and unused markup causes no penalty.
- Measured title/meta CTR lifts (Backlinko, 4M-5M results): question-word titles +14.1%, titles 40-60 chars +8.9%, keyword in URL +45%, meta description present +5.8% more clicks, positive sentiment +4-7%, power words -13.9%. Zyppy: Google rewrites 61% of titles; 51-60 chars has the lowest rewrite rate; bracketed segments get rewritten 77.6% and stripped 32.9% of the time - avoid relying on brackets.
- Freshness: adding the current year to titles plus real content updates lifted CTR 37-640% across 10 articles in one case study; Google's December 2025 core update now detects and discounts date-bumping without substantive changes. LLMs (ChatGPT retrieval) show measurable recency bias, so visible updated dates + dateModified help AI citation too. Daily-changing computed values (e.g., today's date results) are a natural, legitimate freshness signal for a date-calculator site.
- People Also Ask: use the exact question as an H2/H3 with a direct 40-60-word answer immediately below; refresh answers periodically (Google rotates PAA); 63% of PAA engagement is mobile. Semrush's PAA study confirms short, directly-formatted answers under question headings win the boxes.
- Featured snippets: 40-60 words (~300-320 chars), answer-first under a heading that mirrors the query; tables win calculation/comparison queries; pages with images are ~40% likelier to be featured; snippet capture = 42.9% CTR. Caveat: snippet SERP visibility fell 64% (15.41% to 5.53%) Jan-Jun 2025 as AI Overviews displaced them - but snippets remain the single strongest predictor of AI Overview citation, so the same optimization serves both.
- AI Overviews math: their presence cuts top-result CTR by 58% (Ahrefs Dec 2025 update; earlier estimate -34.5%); Pew observed clicks fall from 15% to 8% when an AIO shows, and only 1% of AIO views click a cited source. Only 38% of AIO citations now come from top-10 results (down from 76%), meaning pages outside page one can get cited. Strategic implication for utility sites: interactive tools are defensible because the answer requires interaction; target transactional/tool-intent queries where AIOs appear least.
- GEO traffic is real money now: AI platforms drove 1.13B referral visits in June 2025 (+357% YoY, Similarweb); ChatGPT accounts for ~87.4% of AI referrals (Conductor); ChatGPT sessions convert at 15.9% and Perplexity at 10.5% vs ~1.76% for Google organic (Seer Interactive). Notably, Seer found Gemini conversions concentrated on practical tools and calculator pages, and Similarweb found ChatGPT-recommended sites 2.5x likelier to get a visit within 7 days.
- GEO tactics with measured effect sizes: Princeton/Georgia Tech GEO study - adding citations/sources +30-40% AI visibility, statistics +35-40%, expert quotes +40%; Semrush - Q&A formatting +25.45% citation rate, promotional tone -26.19%. Concrete plumbing: submit to Bing Webmaster Tools + IndexNow (ChatGPT/Copilot pull from Bing's index); allow GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot in robots.txt; llms.txt costs nothing but no major engine has confirmed using it (Google's Mueller says none do).
- Google Discover: eligibility is automatic once indexed and policy-compliant - requires 1200px+ images and max-image-preview:large meta; Discover averaged ~8% CTR vs ~2% in search (NewzDash Q1 2025) and grew 6% while search pageviews fell 9% (Chartbeat). Weak fit for raw calculator pages; the realistic play is the guide/article layer (with large hero images) riding trending or seasonal date topics.
- Net playbook for the datesum-style pipeline: (1) mark every calculator page up as WebApplication + BreadcrumbList and guides as Article; (2) rewrite titles into 40-60-char question forms with the keyword early and current year only where content genuinely updates; (3) add a 40-60-word answer-first paragraph plus a data table above the tool on every page (snippet + AIO + PAA all feed from it); (4) add per-page real Q&A sections with question H2s; (5) register with Bing/IndexNow and open robots.txt to AI crawlers; (6) cite authoritative sources and include concrete statistics in guide content for the 30-40% GEO lift.
